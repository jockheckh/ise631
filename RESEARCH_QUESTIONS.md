# Central research questions (approved)

1. What is gained—and lost—by replacing the exact MDP value function with Adelman's approximate value function?
2. Why does Adelman's approximate LP produce a bound that dominates the deterministic/fluid formulation?
3. How do LP duality, reduced costs, and column generation make the resulting large-scale problem computationally tractable?
4. How does the method scale with increasing state/action-space complexity, and can portions of the problem be truncated without materially changing the solution?
5. How large are the performance gaps among the fluid formulation, approximate LP, dynamic bid-price policy, and exact MDP—and under what conditions do dynamic prices provide the greatest benefit?
6. Can Adelman's framework be meaningfully applied to a surveillance-pricing problem using real or realistically calibrated data?

**Analytical warning:** RQ3 follows approved framing but must be reconciled with the precise separation/cutting-plane/column-generation method actually in the article; do not force a simplex interpretation if the math disagrees.
